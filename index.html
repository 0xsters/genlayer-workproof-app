Write the acceptance criteria in plain language and link the public evidence. Anyone can register a task. Grading happens when someone resolves it.

Tasks

Resolving a task fetches the evidence page and asks GenLayer validators to grade it. The verdict is stored only after validators agree: the verdict must match exactly, the score must be within 15 points, and the criteria met count within 1.

WorkProof is a reusable acceptance primitive for bounties, contribution programs, and agent marketplaces: the party offering work and the party doing it can both point at the same on-chain verdict instead of arguing over screenshots.
Network: GenLayer Studionet (testnet, chain 61999). Contract source and tests: github.com/0xsters/genlayer-workproof-verifier
